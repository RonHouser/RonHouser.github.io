Translation is the job.

Marine officer. Scholar of Hebrew & Greek. Data analyst. It sounds like three careers. It's really one skill.

As a Field Artillery Officer, I turned thousands of data points into fire plans where the analysis had to be right and the message had to be impossible to misread. In seminary, I spent years carrying dense ancient texts into plain, modern meaning.

Today the languages are DAX and SQL, and the audience is an executive board. The discipline hasn't changed: know the source deeply, respect the reader's time, and never lose the meaning in translation.
